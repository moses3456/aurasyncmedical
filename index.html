The problem

Every minute spent searching is a minute away from patients

Across many hospitals, keeping the right supplies in the right place is still largely a manual job. The result is familiar to every nursing unit.

Time lost searching

Nurses often step away from the bedside to look for gloves, dressings, and other everyday items in supply rooms down the hall.

Unexpected stockouts

When an item runs out at the wrong moment, care slows down and teams scramble to borrow from other units.

Waste and overstock

Without a clear picture of what is used, units tend to over-order to stay safe, and supplies can expire unused on the shelf.

Our solution

AuraCart: a smart supply cart that moves with your rounds

AuraCart is a mobile supply cart built for nursing rounds. It travels with nurses from room to room, so the supplies they use most are right where care happens.

Behind the scenes, AuraCart is designed to help supply teams see what each unit needs and restock before it becomes a problem.

  • Close at hand. Everyday supplies travel with the nurse, not down the hall.
  • Clear visibility. Aims to give supply managers a clearer view of unit needs.
  • Simple to use. Designed to feel familiar from the first shift.

FAQ

Questions about the pilot

Can’t find what you are looking for? Email us and we will get back to you.

Does the pilot cost anything?

No. The 12-week pilot is free for selected nursing units. There is no upfront cost and no obligation to continue afterward.

How long does the pilot last?

The pilot runs for 12 weeks. That gives your team enough time to settle into a routine with the cart during real shifts and form a clear view of how it fits your unit.

What do we need to provide?

A nursing unit willing to take part, a main point of contact (often the nurse manager), and a short check-in with your team from time to time. We handle setup and support throughout.

Will it disrupt our current workflow?

It is designed not to. The cart is meant to fit into the rounds your nurses already do, and we plan the setup around your unit’s schedule.

How is our information handled?

Information shared with us during the pilot is used only to run and review the pilot. We do not sell it, and we can work within your facility’s own privacy and security policies. Patient information is not part of the pilot.

Which facilities can apply?

Hospital nursing units, outpatient clinics, and long-term care facilities are all welcome to apply. We select pilot sites based on fit and availability.

What happens after the 12 weeks?

We review the pilot together with your team. If it is a good fit, we will discuss next steps. If not, we collect the cart and there is nothing further to do.
