Why AuraCart

Care stops when supplies go missing

The problem

Searching instead of caring

Nurses routinely leave patient care to search for basic supplies, while hospitals lose significant value every year to expired, overstocked, or missing inventory.

Today, most supply tracking is manual, static, and disconnected from where care actually happens.

The solution

Supplies that keep up with your rounds

AuraCart is a mobile smart supply cart that travels with nurses from room to room. It keeps track of its own inventory in real time and shows stock levels and expiration dates right on the cart.

Nurses always know what they have. Supply teams always know what needs restocking, before it becomes a stockout or waste.

How it works

Three steps. No clipboards.

  1. 01

    Stock it.

    Load the cart with the supplies your unit uses every day.

  2. 02

    It tracks itself.

    AuraCart keeps its own count as items are used, and shows what's on hand and what's expiring soon.

  3. 03

    Your team gets alerts.

    Supply staff hear about low stock and upcoming expirations in time to restock.
